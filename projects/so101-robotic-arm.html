01 Overview

The SO-101 is a servo-driven robotic arm built on Waveshare ST3215 serial bus servos, controlled here with a leader-follower teleoperation setup. Over a 15-day robotics workshop, I recorded a demonstration dataset by teleoperating the follower arm, then trained an ACT policy on it to reproduce the demonstrated motion from camera input alone - with the dataset and trained model published on the Hugging Face Hub.

Per-Frame Structure

  • observation.images.top / observation.images.wrist - 320×240 RGB camera frames.
  • observation.state - 6 current joint positions (follower arm).
  • action - 6 target joint positions, recorded from the leader arm during teleoperation.
  • Per-frame timestamps plus episode and frame indices.

03 Model

Jay1061/so101_act ↗ - an ACT policy that predicts a chunk of future joint actions from current camera images and joint state, reducing compounding error versus single-step prediction.

04 How to Use

Load the Policy

from lerobot.policies.act.modeling_act import ACTPolicy

policy = ACTPolicy.from_pretrained("Jay1061/so101_act")
policy.eval()  # put the model in evaluation mode

Deploying on a real SO-101 arm uses LeRobot's record command with --policy.path=Jay1061/so101_act, following the SO-101 setup guide in the LeRobot docs.

Load the Dataset

from lerobot.datasets.lerobot_dataset import LeRobotDataset

dataset = LeRobotDataset("Jay1061/record-test")

print("Episodes:", dataset.num_episodes)
print("Frames:", dataset.num_frames)

# Look at the first frame
first_frame = dataset[0]
print(first_frame.keys())
